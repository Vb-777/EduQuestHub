About EduQuestHub

What is EduQuestHub?

EduQuestHub is a learning portal built as part of the PG Software Lab course at IIT Ropar (2023). It brings together everything a computer-science student needs in one place: curated video lectures for 8 core GATE CSE subjects, nine years of previous GATE papers, hands-on PG Software Lab topics (LaTeX, Git, AWK, web scraping, tries, transfer learning, LSTM & GloVe) and daily MCQ quizzes.

The long-term goal is an exam-preparation platform that analyses quiz performance, highlights strong and weak topics, and recommends a study path.
